# Marketing Plan: First 3 Paying Clients

**Log line:** Find 50 local plumbers, show each one what their callers hear when nobody picks up, and sign 3 of them in 2 weeks.

**The one-line pitch:** You already paid to make the phone ring. We make sure someone answers.

**What we sell (say only this, nothing more):** When you miss a call, our assistant picks up, gets the caller's name, number, address and problem, and sends it to your WhatsApp within a minute. You call them back before they call someone else.

> Be honest about what it does today: it takes details and alerts the owner. It does **not** book into a calendar yet. Never promise booking.

---

## 1. First niche: plumbers

| | Plumbers | Roofers | HVAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calls are urgent | Yes, leaks and clogs can't wait | Mostly not (except storms) | Yes in peak heat/cold |
| Calls all year | Yes | Storm and season spikes | Two busy seasons |
| Owner is often on the tools | Yes, under a sink | On a roof | In an attic |
| Missed caller just calls the next one | Yes, right away | Often waits | Yes in peak season |
| Many small 1-5 truck shops | Very many | Many | Fewer; bigger shops already pay answering services |

**Pick plumbers.** The caller has water on the floor, so they don't leave a voicemail, they call the next name on Google. That makes the pain easy to see and easy to explain. Small shops are run by the owner, who also answers the phone, so you talk straight to the person who pays.

Stay with plumbers until you have 3 paying clients. Roofers are the second niche (good fit after a storm). HVAC is third.

## 2. The offer and price

**Free 14-day pilot, then monthly.** No contract.

| | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free, 14 days | Real forwarding on their real phone. You set it all up. |
| Setup fee | $199 | **Waived for the pilot clients.** Charge it from client 4 onward. |
| Monthly | $249/month | Covers the number, the AI minutes and the WhatsApp alerts. Cancel any time. |

Why this works:
- One saved plumbing job pays for the month. Let the owner do that math with their own job price; don't quote numbers for them.
- Free pilot removes the risk. At the end you show them a plain list: every call it caught, with name, number and problem.
- No contract, so "yes" is easy.

**The pilot deal (say it the same way every time):** "Try it free for 14 days. At the end I'll show you every call it caught. If it caught nothing worth calling back, you owe nothing and I turn it off. If it did, it's $249 a month and you can stop any time."

Before the pilot ends, check your own costs for that client (Vapi minutes + Twilio) in the admin page so you know the $249 leaves a profit.

## 3. Finding the first 50 plumbers

Pick your own area first (561 / Palm Beach County), then the next county over. Local is easier: "I'm in Boynton too" opens doors.

**Where to look:**
1. **Google Maps:** search "plumber near [town]". Go town by town. Skip the big names with 50+ trucks and a call center. You want shops with 10-150 reviews and the owner's name in the reviews ("Mike was great").
2. **Reviews that mention phones:** sort reviews by newest and look for "couldn't reach them", "never called back", "went to voicemail". Mark those as hot.
3. **Local Facebook groups:** "[Town] Neighbors", "[Town] Moms", home-owner groups. Search "plumber" and see who gets recommended. Small owner-run shops show up here.
4. **Nextdoor, Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack:** same names, more owner names and cell numbers.
5. **Supply houses:** Ferguson, local plumbing supply. Go early (6:30-8am). Plumbers stand in line there. Bring a card with your demo number.
6. **Business Facebook pages:** many small shops post "call or text" with the owner's cell.

**Outreach list template** (Google Sheet, one row per business):

| Business | Owner | Phone | Town | Source | Reviews | Phone complaint in reviews? | After-hours test (date/time, what happened) | Contacted (date, how) | Reply | Next step | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ABC Plumbing | Dave | 561-555-0100 | Boynton | Google Maps | 74 | Yes, "no call back" | Tue 7:40pm, voicemail full | Wed call, no answer; Wed text | "Send info" | Call Thu 7am | Warm |

Status words: New, Tested, Contacted, Warm, Demo done, Pilot, Paying, No.

## 4. Scripts (word for word)

### The demo hook: call their business after hours

This is your best opener. It uses their own phone, not a stat.

1. Call each plumber on your list between 6pm and 9pm, or on a Saturday morning.
2. Write down what happens: rings out, voicemail, voicemail full, a person, an answering service.
3. If a person answers: say "Sorry, wrong number" and mark them low. They already answer.
4. If it goes to voicemail: hang up (no message). That's your opening line tomorrow.

Then you say: *"I called your shop last night at 7:40 and got voicemail. If I'd had a leak, I'd have called the next plumber. Want to hear what your callers could hear instead?"* and give them your demo number.

**Your demo number:** your own agent, set up as a made-up plumbing shop ("Palm Coast Plumbing"). When they call it, it should sound like their own receptionist. Then show them the WhatsApp that lands on your phone, live, while you're on the call. That moment sells it.

### Cold call (morning, 7-8am, before they go out)

> "Hi, is this Dave? This is Mike, I'm local here in Boynton. I'll be quick.
>
> I called your shop last night around 7:40 and it went to voicemail. Not a knock, everybody's on a job or off for the night.
>
> I set up a phone assistant for plumbers. When you can't pick up, it answers, gets the caller's name, address and problem, and sends it to your WhatsApp in about a minute. So you call them back before they call someone else.
>
> I'm doing a free 14-day trial for a few local shops. Can I text you a number to call so you can hear it yourself? Takes one minute."

If yes: text the demo number right away. Call back that afternoon: "Did you get a chance to call it?"

If "I'm busy": "Totally. Can I text you the demo number and call you tomorrow at 7?"

### Text (only after a call attempt, one text, to the business number)

> Hi Dave, this is Mike, local in Boynton. I called your shop last night at 7:40 and got voicemail. I set up a phone assistant that answers when you can't and sends you the caller's details on WhatsApp in a minute. Free 14-day trial. Call this number to hear it: [demo number]. Reply STOP and I won't text again.

Rules: one text per business, never a blast. Only to the number they publish for the business. Always include the STOP line. If they don't answer, call once more, then leave them.

### Facebook / Instagram DM

> Hey Dave, Mike here, I'm local in Boynton. Quick one: I called your shop last night after 7 and got voicemail. Totally normal, but those callers usually try the next plumber. I set up a phone assistant that answers when you can't and sends you their name, address and problem on WhatsApp in about a minute. Want to hear it? Call [demo number] and pretend you've got a leak.

### Supply house / in person

> "You guys ever miss calls while you're on a job? I set up a thing that answers for you and texts you the caller's details. Here's a number, call it and pretend you've got a leak. Free 2-week trial if you like it."

Hand them a card with the demo number and your cell.

### Questions to ask once they're interested

- About how many calls a week? How many do you think you miss?
- Who answers now? What happens after 6pm and on weekends?
- Do you pay for Google ads, Angi or Thumbtack?
- What's a normal job worth to you? (Let *them* say the number.)
- Do you use WhatsApp? (If not, SMS works as backup.)

## 5. Two-week launch checklist

Goal: 3 pilots started in week 1-2, 3 paying when their pilots end.

**Before Day 1 (must be done):** worker deployed and tested per [RUN-GUIDE.md](RUN-GUIDE.md); demo agent ("Palm Coast Plumbing") live; you have called it 5 times and the WhatsApp arrived every time.

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 (Mon) | Build the sheet. Add 25 plumbers from Google Maps in your town and the next. Note phone complaints in reviews. |
| 2 | Add 25 more (Facebook groups, Nextdoor, Yelp). Evening 6-9pm: after-hours test call all 50. Write down what happened. |
| 3 | 7-8am: cold call the 15 that went to voicemail. Text the ones who didn't pick up. Evening: test any you missed. |
| 4 | 6:30am: one supply house, hand out 10 cards. 7-8am: call the next 15. Afternoon: call back anyone who got the demo. |
| 5 | Calls to the rest. DM the ones with Facebook pages. Goal by tonight: 5 people have called the demo. |
| 6 (Sat) | Saturday-morning after-hours test on anyone not yet tested. Set up pilot #1 if you have a yes (forwarding test together on the phone). |
| 7 (Sun) | Rest. Update the sheet. Write down which line got the most replies. |
| 8 | Follow-up calls to every "Warm". Set up pilots #2 and #3 if ready. If not: add 25 more plumbers (next town). |
| 9 | Second supply house. New after-hours tests on the new 25. |
| 10 | Call the new 25. Check pilot clients: send each a short WhatsApp: "Here's what it caught so far." |
| 11 | Ask every "No" one question: "What would have made it a yes?" Write the answers down. |
| 12 | Fill any open pilot spot. Fix anything a pilot client complained about. |
| 13 | Pilot #1 ends soon: book a 10-minute call. Show the list of caught calls. Ask: "Want to keep it at $249 a month?" Send the payment link. |
| 14 | Same for any other pilot near its end. Review: which source, which script, which town worked best. Do more of that. |

**Rule:** at least 20 new contacts every weekday. If you only do one thing a day, make it the 7am calls.

**Getting paid:** use a Stripe payment link for $249/month (cancel any time). If they don't pay, `node scripts/client.mjs pause their-business`.

**After a client pays:** ask "Do you know another plumber, or a roofer or electrician, who misses calls?" A name from a happy client is worth ten cold calls. Real reviews and quotes only from real clients, with their OK.

## 6. Objections and answers

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I don't miss calls." | "Good. Then it costs you nothing to try for 2 weeks and see. I called last night at 7:40 and got voicemail, so let's check." |
| "Callers leave a voicemail." | "Some do. Most people with a leak don't, they call the next plumber. This one gets their details every time." |
| "People hate talking to robots." | "Call it and judge for yourself. It's short and polite, it just takes their details. Better than a beep." |
| "I already have an answering service." | "Then you're covered. If it ever costs more than $249 or misses calls, keep my number." |
| "Too expensive." | "What's one normal job worth to you? If it catches one job a month you'd have lost, it paid for itself. And the first 2 weeks are free." |
| "I don't have time to set it up." | "It takes 10 minutes on the phone with me. You turn on 'forward when no answer' and we test it together. I do the rest." |
| "Can it book jobs into my calendar?" | "Not yet. It gets the details and sends them to you right away, so you decide who to call first." |
| "What if it says something wrong?" | "It only takes details. It doesn't quote prices or promise times. I'll set the words with you before it goes live." |
| "Send me info." | "Sure. Easiest info is to call [demo number] for one minute. Can I call you tomorrow at 7 to hear what you thought?" |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What's the part you're not sure about?" (Then answer that one thing.) |
| "Is this a scam / who are you?" | "Fair question. I'm Mike, local in [town]. No contract, no card for the trial. If you don't like it, I turn it off." |

## Rules for all copy

- No fake reviews, quotes or "trusted by 100 plumbers".
- No fake rush ("only 2 spots left" when it isn't true).
- No made-up stats or years in ads, posts or texts. Use their own phone: "I called you and got voicemail" beats any number.
- Say only what it does today: answers, takes details, sends WhatsApp.
